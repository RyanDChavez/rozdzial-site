Privacy Policy

Rozdział works without any account. The app has no advertising, tracking, or analytics and sends no manuscripts or usage data to the developer.

Manuscripts and backups

Rozdział saves your manuscript, notes, versions, and writing progress in the file you choose. If you save to iCloud Drive or another storage provider, that provider handles storage and sync under its own privacy policy. Rozdział can keep recovery copies on this device and backups on your Mac or in a folder you choose. Recovery copies stay on the device until removed from its storage.

You can delete manuscripts and backups in Files or Finder. Copies you saved or exported separately are separate files. Your storage provider may keep deleted files or older versions under its own retention settings. To stop syncing a manuscript, move it to local storage and remove any cloud copies you no longer want.

Support and TestFlight

If you email support, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include. We use it to respond and keep it as long as needed to handle your request. If you test through TestFlight, Apple may share tester contact information, usage information, crash reports, and any feedback you submit. We use this information to manage beta access and investigate issues. Apple explains how it handles this information in its TestFlight privacy notice.

To ask about privacy or request deletion of information you sent us, email support@rozdzial.com.
